Abstract

World models provide predictive visual features. Directed Temporal Representations for Control (DTRC) organizes these features around the cost of reaching a goal. From offline trajectories, DTRC learns a temporal distance in environment steps, allowing forward and reverse costs to differ. The temporal critic guides action learning through recorded behavior and model-predicted improvements. We study the resulting geometry and control performance across ten visual tasks.
